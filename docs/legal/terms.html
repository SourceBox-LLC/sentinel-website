  • Command Center: our hosted dashboard and API. It relays live video to the members of your organization, sends notifications, and provides incidents, the optional Sentinel AI agent, the Model Context Protocol (MCP) server, and the Home Assistant integration.

"Service" in these Terms means Command Center as we host it, together with the licence and cloud-sync services we run for self-hosted installs. 6.Sentinel AI

Sentinel AI is an optional agent on paid plans and on self-hosted installs with a licence key. It is off until an admin of your organization turns it on. While it is on, it can be started by motion, by a new incident, or by hand. It looks at your cameras, can save snapshots and short clips as evidence, and writes incident reports.

To do this, Sentinel AI sends camera images, camera names, and incident text to a third-party AI model provider. The current provider is named in the Privacy Policy, and we will give notice before changing it. By turning Sentinel AI on, you instruct us to send that data on your behalf, and you confirm that doing so is lawful for your cameras.

AI output can be wrong. Sentinel AI can misidentify people, objects, and events, and can miss them entirely. Treat its reports as suggestions to check, not as facts. Do not use them as the only basis for decisions that affect someone's rights, such as accusing, reporting, disciplining, or excluding a person.

Each plan includes a monthly number of Sentinel AI runs. When it is used up, new runs pause until the next month; nothing else is affected.
